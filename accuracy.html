Scoring method

We applied the comparison's stated rules to this forecast, so the numbers line up with the other six models.

  • Results: AP race calls from the Boston Globe elections feed, as of November 4, 20:10 UTC. AP had called 403 of the 470 House and Senate races.
  • Uncalled races: scored by the current vote leader, except the seven the comparison fixed. NV-02, FL-09, AZ-02, NC-03, AK-SEN and MS-SEN count as Democratic wins; WA-05 counts as Republican.
  • Exclusions: Louisiana's two unresolved races (LA-05 runoff, LA-06 uncalled) and California's same-party contests.
  • Measures: Brier score is the mean of (p − outcome)². Log loss uses natural logs, with p clipped to 0.5%–99.5%. A race at 50% or below counts as a Republican pick.
  • Probabilities: each race's Democratic win chance in the final November 3 forecast (10,000 simulations).

The feed shows nine California same-party races, against the comparison's six, which leaves 459 races. Adding back any three of the nine gives 462 and the figures above. On 459 the figures are Brier 0.0407 and log loss 0.1325, and the ranking holds.

How to verify

  1. Download the file and its proof (links above).
  2. Check the fingerprint: shasum -a 256 forecast_2026-11-03.json should print the SHA-256 above.
  3. Check the proof with the OpenTimestamps client: pip install opentimestamps-client, then ots verify forecast_2026-11-03.json.ots. It reports Bitcoin block 974389. A full check needs a Bitcoin node; without one, ots info forecast_2026-11-03.json.ots lists the proof's steps.
  4. Those steps end at merkle root 951d10e3a5f0fa626adf26c1ffc468c4bc2e7bdf4c121652a5ed75e2566aad5a, which matches block 974389 on any block explorer.

Earlier versions from the same day are stamped too and listed in SHA256SUMS: a first run (block 974381, 10:14 UTC) and a run with duplicate generic-ballot polls removed (block 974389). The final version corrected one poll, where Wikipedia had swapped the two candidates' numbers in Emerson's Ohio Senate poll.

Caveats

  • Provisional results. 66 races were uncalled at scoring time, and several are within half a point: NC-05, MN-01, AZ-02, TN-04 and FL-09. The scores will move as counting finishes.
  • Race set. The stamped file lists the 157 races the site shows individually. The other 302 scored races are safe House seats, with odds from the same November 3 model run. All but 8 were below 2% or above 98%, and all were called correctly.
  • Same-party exclusions. Our count of California same-party contests (nine) differs from the comparison's six. Every way of picking three to add back gives the same scores.
